# H01A CRT 预现 v0.1 提示词

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: release-level 16:9 survival-horror key frame, close environmental shot establishing an analog CRT that shows a biological event before it exists in reality

Input images: Image 1 is an environment reference for the same late-1990s Chinese coastal hotel lobby, architecture, materials, fog, red carpet and warm/cold lighting. Image 2 is a subject-development reference only for the scale and functional hotel-organ idea; do not copy its wing-like silhouette, excessive tendons or exact pose. Generate a new camera composition rather than editing either reference literally.

Primary request: Inside the post-crisis empty hotel lobby around the year 2000, place an old 4:3 curved-glass CRT security monitor in extreme foreground on the reception side cabinet, occupying roughly the left 35–40 percent of the widescreen frame. The real lobby remains clearly visible beyond and beside the monitor. The CRT displays the same lobby from a fixed high corner CCTV camera, but shows the hotel’s unfinished developmental organ in a later closed configuration that does not yet exist in physical reality.

Real-space background: The same four-storey coastal county-hotel atrium is empty of ordinary people. Red carpet, wooden front desk, brass and stone details, balconies, chandelier, one abandoned suitcase, fallen bouquet and an off-hook telephone remain. Natural sea fog is thin at ground level and progressively denser upward. The main red-carpet route is physically empty—no monster body, plates or tendons have emerged yet.

Three pre-development traces in reality:
1. A red fire-water pipe on a cream column is covered in condensation beads visibly traveling upward, converging on one coupling.
2. Three elevator steel cables in a recessed rear opening are pulled unusually taut and one cable shows a slight motion blur from a single vibration, although the elevator doors are closed.
3. Sea fog in the middle of the empty lobby bends around a broad concave pressure wake shaped by something large but completely invisible; chandelier chains and loose fabric lean around the same absent volume.

CRT future image: On the curved screen, show a distinctly different, later state of this exact lobby. A coherent asymmetric biological apparatus is anchored to floor, column and elevator shaft. It has no head or full animal body: three pale mineral support plates, a central offset joint with two limited translucent hydraulic sacs, three major tendon bundles using elevator cables, and one irregular multi-layer filtering fan folded across the red-carpet route. The future organ must be unmistakable on the small screen through a strong dark silhouette and pale plate edges, without requiring fine detail. It occupies the same area that is visibly empty behind the monitor in reality.

Analog screen treatment: Authentic 1998–2001 closed-circuit CRT image—low resolution, interlaced horizontal fields, mild phosphor bloom, curved glass distortion, weak desaturated green-grey color, slight vertical roll at one edge and natural analog smear on moving filtering lamellae. No digital pixel glitch, RGB block corruption, VHS text, timestamp, camera label or readable UI. The image is surprisingly stable and coherent rather than chaotic static.

Temporal contradiction: Align the CRT and background composition so the viewer can compare them instantly: a recognizable chandelier, column, red-carpet edge and elevator opening appear both on-screen and in reality. On-screen the organ has already closed around them; beyond the screen those locations remain empty but show the three precursor traces. This comparison is the main horror event.

Additional unsettling detail: The CRT screen’s convex glass faintly reflects the real empty lobby. Within that reflection only, the invisible pressure wake has a darker continuous body contour than in reality, but keep it subtle. Do not add a human reflection, face or eye.

Composition: Human eye height close to the front desk, approximately 35–40 mm lens. CRT foreground edge, real-space pipe/elevator and distant fog wake create a triangular visual path. Keep enough real lobby visible to understand the spatial match. The monitor should feel heavy, old, dusty and physically connected by coaxial cables, not a floating UI panel.

Lighting and mood: Weak warm tungsten from front desk and chandelier grazes the CRT casing and wet pipe; cold grey-green ambient fog fills the atrium. Screen light softly illuminates nearby wood and the off-hook receiver. Deep but readable shadows, physically plausible wet speculars, no global horror filter.

Materials: Yellowed ABS monitor casing, scratched convex glass, dust caught at ventilation slots, worn varnished wood, damp carpet fibers, oxidized pipe joints, wet stone, authentic analog cable and BNC-style connection without emphasizing modern technical detail.

Post-crisis rule: No ordinary people, wedding crowd, survivor, corpse, human sculpture or human-shaped monster anywhere. The organ exists only inside the CRT in this shot. Physical reality contains only material precursors and invisible pressure.

Constraints: Photoreal live-action practical-set credibility; Chinese coastal hotel around 2000; direct visual comparison between screen future and empty reality; no copied Alien/Giger silhouette. No readable text, timestamp, logo, watermark, caption or HUD.

Avoid: modern flat screen, computer monitor, security-control-room wall of screens, digital glitch art, scanline overlay covering entire image, ghost face on TV, static-only screen, jump-scare face, living person in background, generic haunted hotel, blue underwater filter, gore, black goo, tentacles, eggs, xenomorph, elongated head, complete alien creature, floating rings, feathered wing, steampunk machine, robot, excessive cables, low-poly render, collage, split-screen composition.

Pass conditions: At thumbnail size the viewer must immediately understand that the CRT shows the same lobby occupied by a future biological structure while the real lobby behind it is still empty. On enlargement, upward pipe condensation, taut elevator cable and invisible fog wake must support—not replace—that contradiction.
