# v0.4 校准提示词

## 全局前缀（每张图都使用）

Production concept art for a grounded 2001 Chinese coastal-town survival-horror game called Retide (返潮). This is a deliberate art-directed frame, not a random mood image and not a disaster photograph. Use a controlled palette: 60% cool cement mist gray, 20% oxidized teal-green, 10% faded workwear blue, 7% warm shell-ash bone, 3% muted alarm red. Keep large clean areas and strong value separation. Fear comes from one impossible physical or human action, never from grime density. Real camera lens, believable Chinese coastal architecture, restrained PS3-era horror composition, high detail on the focal subject, simple background shapes, readable at 720p. No blue underwater filter, no gray monochrome wash, no dirty-lens effect, no clutter, no decorative ruins, no slime, no tentacles, no aliens, no infection trope, no generic sea monster, no gore, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## 01 校准图：归面转向陆地

[Use the global prefix.] Landscape 1536x1024, 35mm eye-level wide shot from the town side of a nearly empty tidal flat. Six family members stand in a straight line with their backs to camera, still facing the sea. Six warm shell-ash clay effigies stand ten meters in front of them, all facing the camera and therefore the town. The figures are separated by clean pale mud and quiet negative space; no crowd. One concrete warning tower sits on the far right with one small muted red lamp. No wave arrives. The only impossible event is the synchronized wrong direction of the six effigies. Use bone-warm effigies against cool gray-green land; keep workwear blue distinct. The image must be eerie before it is dirty, and the viewer must be able to say: the living are sending them away, but the returned faces already face home.

## 02 英雄恐怖图：无正面父亲

[Use the global prefix.] Landscape 1536x1024, 50mm medium-long shot in a clean concrete fish-processing corridor. The camera sees the back of one ordinary father in a faded blue work jacket, left shoulder lowered, right foot half-dragging. A hanging stainless panel gives only a narrow side reflection. In that reflection, there is no face: a warm shell-bone fossil plate runs from forehead to abdomen and contains exactly three small closed stone eyes. Keep the front mostly hidden. One muted red alarm reflection touches the floor; all other color is restrained. Make the back unmistakably human and the side reveal unmistakably wrong. No three-headed creature, no full shell armor, no wetness, no barnacle collage, no exposed organs.

## 03 玩法图：警报测深

[Use the global prefix.] Landscape 1536x1024, third-person game camera inside a small concrete warning-tower room. The player is a young outsider in a dark blue work jacket, left of frame, hand on one old metal control box. Through the open doorway, a pale mudflat and a single distant father silhouette are visible. The short alarm creates exactly one narrow vertical column where dust falls, a cloth strip points straight down and the door hinge compresses; outside the column everything remains still. The column is a clear visual route barrier. Put one muted red lamp above the box, one oxidized teal cable, and warm shell-bone dust in the column. No HUD, no magic lines, no enemy vision, no combat.

## 04 材质与色光校准

[Use the global prefix.] Landscape 1536x1024, six clean material vignettes arranged with generous spacing on a cool cement-gray board: a warm shell-bone clay effigy with one wrong human pressure groove; oxidized teal alarm-box metal pulled inward on a single straight line; faded blue work cloth falling normally except one section pointing down; pale shell-fossil with three closed stone eyes; one black-green mold seam growing upward; warm dust column in dry air. Add one small muted red light source crossing the bottom edge. Each vignette has one focal anomaly and a calm field around it. No collage of dirt, no labels, no wet mud, no monochrome gray, no generic ocean texture.
