Primary request: Transform this from an unnatural hard fog shelf with living wedding guests into the clearly post-crisis version of the same hotel: no normal people anywhere in the physical lobby or live mirror reflection, only traces of their abrupt disappearance and one possible monster.

In the mirror, its otherwise human silhouette subtly continues sideways behind two adjacent doorway reflections, suggesting one continuous body occupying impossible room length. No crowd, no ghost overlays.

Returning-damp correction: Strengthen the crisis without making flesh walls. Across the left load-bearing column and one upper wall, create broad structural-scale edema swelling over several metres, crossing panel joints and a corner as if trapped water pressure is bowing the architecture inward. Keep the original cream paint, tile base and construction seams recognizable. Add irregular black damp speckles rising from column bases and carpet edges along believable capillary routes, densest higher up but not spread uniformly. Show thin water films moving upward along the column, mirror frame, telephone cable and chandelier chain. The floor remains only thinly wet, not flooded.

Creature and negative-space event: Preserve one distant human-scale back-facing figure as the only active subject. Fog bends around an empty volume much larger than its visible body, forming a subtle moving cavity toward the upper balcony. This is evidence of the same spatially extended creature, not a second monster.

Composition: Keep the red carpet route leading toward the figure. Use the empty lobby, progressively dense vertical fog, stopped footprints, mirror discrepancy and single figure to create visual tension. The upper floors must still read as a tall atrium before dissolving into fog.

Lighting/mood: Electricity still works unevenly. Preserve warm tungsten and muted red-gold millennium hotel colors on the lower floor, colder grey-green fog above, readable deep shadows and wet specular detail. Slight practical flicker is implied, not a global dark filter.
