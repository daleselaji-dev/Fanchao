# 食堂一张图

## 要让人先看到什么

一间正在营业的 2001 年国营渔业食堂侧间。没有水，没有雾，没有蓝光，也没有人正在受害。

靠近地面的半截房间已经不属于陆地上的时间：两张桌子的桌面还摆着搪瓷碗，桌腿却短了几厘米；铁皮柜的下半扇门被长期压得略向内弯，门缝里挤出旧胶条；完整的乳白墙面在同一高度以下，墙角积着一层干燥、压实的鱼鳞、纤维和铁屑。所有变化都在同一个高度开始，但那条高度本身看不见。

## 画面不能变成什么

- 不能是房间进水、下雨、漏水或潮湿事故。
- 不能是熔化的桌椅、波浪状墙面、蓝色滤镜或发光边界。
- 不能靠海星、珊瑚、触手、鱼群、霉菌或器官填空。
- 不能把时间做成废墟感；上半部仍要有人每天擦拭、使用和维修。
- 不能把异常刚好放在原有的腰线、瓷砖接缝或家具边缘上。

## 生成或搭建时只验证三件事

1. 人能否先认出这是一个真实、具体、仍在使用的单位食堂？
2. 人能否不靠文字，看出有一个绝对水平但不可见的高度穿过房间？
3. 异常能否读成漫长压力留下的后果，而不是灾害特效？

三项中有一项不成立，就不进入 45 秒原型。

Asset type: one 16:9 target frame for a third-person console horror game environment, not a poster
Primary request: A dry side room of a small state-owned fishery canteen in northern China, year 2001, still serving lunch. The room is clean enough to be in use: a fluorescent tube is on, warm service-window light falls on a just-set-down enamel bowl, an order slip and chopsticks are on two square wooden tables. No protagonist and no posed people in frame.

The impossible fact: an invisible perfectly level sea height crosses an uninterrupted off-white plaster wall and the middle of a plain grey metal storage cabinet, about one metre above the floor. Nothing draws the line. Above that height every material is ordinary. Below that height, the wooden table legs are a few centimetres shorter and minutely bent only along believable load paths; the cabinet door edges are thickened, its old rubber gasket is squeezed out, and dry compacted fish scales, fibres and iron dust are trapped in the baseboard corners. It must look like these lower parts spent decades under immense deep-sea pressure while the room remained completely dry and operational.

Camera and mood: eye-level wide interior shot, 35 mm lens, enough unbroken wall and cabinet surface to prove the identical height; muted winter daylight plus ordinary fluorescent light; dense, tactile early-2000s construction materials; restrained PS3-era horror-game environment quality, ordinary life made physically wrong.

Constraints: no water, puddles, rain, wet floor, fog, blue lighting, visible waterline, glow, magical barrier, cracked disaster scene, melted furniture, wavy walls, sea creatures, coral, tentacles, mould, organs, corpse, monster, cult decoration, exaggerated ruin, text, logo, watermark. The abnormal parts must not align with tile trim, a wall seam, a window sill, or any existing architectural division.
