# H01 雾顶大堂 v0.1 图像生成提示词

## 生成目的

验证阈限酒店能否在第一张画面中同时成立：千禧海镇的真实酒店、提前废墟化、室内海雾奇观、镜中婚宴、返潮物质异变与首个实体的微弱预告。

本图不是最终游戏资产，不直接进入引擎；它是后续 Blender 场景、预渲染背景板、灯光和特效拆分的发布级目标帧。

## 使用方式

Use case: photorealistic-natural

Asset type: widescreen release-level environment target frame for a survival horror game, H01 hotel lobby opening shot

Primary request: Create a highly photorealistic, live-action-feeling wide shot of a Chinese coastal county-town hotel lobby around the year 2000, already caught inside an impossible “returning damp” crisis. The image must work as a memorable visual spectacle at thumbnail size, not merely as a damp abandoned interior.

Scene/backdrop: A four-storey atrium lobby in a locally prestigious but modest late-1990s Chinese seaside hotel. Real period details: red-brown and green polished stone, brass railings, smoked mirror wall, slightly cheap crystal chandelier, cream wall panels, patterned carpet, plastic wedding flowers, red fabric remnants, CRT televisions and a wooden reception desk. Avoid Western luxury-palace architecture.

Primary visual phenomenon: A perfectly level horizontal sea-fog plane cuts across the atrium between the second and third floors like a suspended indoor sea surface with no water. Everything above this boundary disappears into dense grey-white fog. The chandelier chain enters the fog and its upper section drifts upward; condensation beads crawl upward along metal. The fog has a sharp flat pressure front rather than soft decorative mist.

Mirror event: A large smoked mirror wall reflects the same lobby filled with an ongoing millennium-era wedding banquet—warm lights, crowded round tables, pale chair covers, red decorations and moving guests—while the physical lobby in front of it is nearly empty and prematurely ruined. The reflected crowd should read clearly as another active state of the same architecture, not as transparent ghost overlays. Some reflected guests turn aside to make a path for someone absent from the real lobby. No readable signage.

Returning-damp material event: One load-bearing wall and attached column have developed a broad structural-scale edema swelling across joints and corners, as if tonnes of water are trapped behind paint. It is not a cluster of round bubbles and not flesh. Stretched wet paint becomes slightly translucent, revealing fragments of the building’s older guesthouse structure underneath. Dense 2–8 mm black tide pores, thin salt crust and tiny closed shell calcifications gather only at the deepest pressure crease, creating one concentrated trypophobic colony rather than dots everywhere. A thin film on the floor runs upward onto the column.

Creature foreshadowing: At the far entrance to a guest corridor, include one small, plausible human figure seen only from behind, wearing ordinary damp hotel-guest clothing. In the mirror, its silhouette is subtly too long and appears to continue behind more than one doorway, but do not reveal the full monster yet. It must create doubt rather than become the central giant subject.

Composition/framing: 16:9 cinematic wide angle from just inside the main entrance, human eye height, approximately a 28–32 mm lens. A wet red carpet and reception geometry lead the eye toward the mirror and the distant corridor figure. The fog plane creates a powerful horizontal cut; the swollen column creates an opposing vertical mass. Keep navigable floor space legible for game production. Strong foreground, midground and background separation.

Lighting/mood: Electricity still works. Warm tungsten chandelier and wedding-reflection light below, cold grey daylight and sea fog above, a few sickly fluorescent practicals. Realistic exposure, deep but readable shadows, wet specular highlights, no cinematic teal-orange grade. The space must feel inhabited minutes ago and ruined years too early.

Color palette: faded warm reds, nicotine cream, aged brass, green-grey stone, cold desaturated sea fog, isolated wet near-black details.

Materials/textures: physically convincing worn stone, cheap varnished wood, oxidized brass, damp carpet fibers, stretched painted plaster, condensation, salt bloom, imperfect late-1990s construction and maintenance. Preserve high-frequency detail only near focal material events; do not cover every surface with noise.

Constraints: Photorealistic practical-set credibility; Chinese coastal hotel around 2000; visual impact comes from spatial contradiction, scale, material pressure and the active mirror world. Keep the lobby mostly dry except for thin damp films. The mirror reflection must obey the same architecture and camera perspective. No HUD, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no readable text.

Avoid: generic abandoned-hotel photography, generic liminal/backrooms corridor, empty beige rooms, Western haunted mansion, opulent fantasy hotel, total flooding, giant waves, blue underwater filter, random barnacles everywhere, seaweed and fish scattered through the lobby, tentacles, black goo, blood, gore, zombies, ghost girl, long-haired woman, antlers, literal human faces in walls, round balloon-like wall blisters, collage aesthetics, symmetrical concept-art showroom, overly clean 3D render, low-poly game screenshot.

## 通过标准

- 缩略图首先读到“水平雾面切断四层大堂”和“镜中仍在举行婚宴”；
- 放大后才读到墙体浮肿、黑潮孔、上行水珠与远处人影；
- 画面不依赖血、怪物大脸或全场海洋装饰；
- 建筑能够拆成真实的游戏空间，而不是无法行走的概念拼贴；
- 即使遮住文字说明，仍然不可能被误认成普通废墟酒店照片。
