# 四张评审图提示词

统一约束：横向 1536x1024；无文字、无 Logo、无水印；可信的 2001 年中国海边小镇；高端 PS3 时代恐怖游戏概念图；水泥灰、褪色警示红、旧工作服蓝、贝灰、黑绿色霉层；不用蓝色水下滤镜、黏液覆盖、通用海怪、感染者、外星或酒店器官。

## 01 滩涂公共送面全景

Use case: stylized-concept. Wide cinematic establishing shot for the horror game Retide, 2001 coastal China. At a broad low-tide mudflat, many families place handmade clay effigies of relatives who died at sea. A concrete warning tower with old loudspeakers and a flashing faded red lamp stands beyond the seawall. The tide has not arrived, yet every effigy is suddenly flattened as if by immense unseen depth and then turns toward land. Dust, loose cloth and door frames in the distant sheds bend toward the town. Families are small fog silhouettes, leaving the foreground readable: cracked mud, shell ash, old cotton clothes, half-dried clay figures. The image must communicate in one glance: they meant to send loved ones away, but the sea is sending mistaken versions home. Grounded 2001 realism, restrained horror, practical materials, no fantasy gloss, no gore.

## 02 无正面父亲角色设计

Use case: stylized-concept. Character design sheet for one creature, front/back/side views on a plain dirty cement-gray studio background. The returned figure is recognized first as a specific Chinese coastal worker father: faded blue work jacket, flattened left shoulder from a fishing net, one dragging right foot, worn trousers and rubber shoes. His back remains fully human and carries household traces. He never turns around. From forehead to abdomen, the front is not a face but one continuous fossilized shell plate filled with small stone-like compound eyes; no mouth, no tentacles, no exposed organs. The shell is a correction made from sea-floor material to fill the family's missing memory of his face. Show dry cracked clay, shell ash and black-green mold at seams, with subtle compression damage. High-end PS3 horror character concept art, readable silhouette, no blue underwater filter, no generic sea monster, no alien, no infection trope, no gore, no text.

## 03 警报测深游戏画面

Use case: ui-mockup plus stylized-concept. Third-person gameplay screenshot from a 2001 coastal warning-tower mudflat. The young outsider alarm operator stands beside a chipped metal control box, hand on a short test-alarm switch. The sound wave is not shown as a sci-fi HUD: instead dust drops in a thin line, a hanging cloth pulls downward, and the nearest door frame visibly compresses inward. A clear short echo leaves a shallow route across dry mud; a second darker route is stretched and lowered, suggesting dangerous depth. In the far fog, the familiar back of a father-shaped returner moves backward toward the active alarm box without turning. The tower, cables, red warning lamp and dry concrete must read at 720p. Grounded PS3-era horror gameplay realism, no enemy-vision overlay, no blue water filter, no combat UI, no text or watermark.

## 04 返潮材质与色光样张

Use case: stylized-concept material board. Four to six close-up panels showing the Retide material language: dry cracked tidal mud with embedded shell ash; old warning-box metal pulled inward and salt-crystallized without water droplets; worn blue work cloth under invisible pressure; fossil shell plate with small compound eyes; black-green mold growing only in seams; pale dust suspended in a dry room. Include a small lighting strip from overcast cement daylight to a single faded red alarm lamp. Materials must feel tangible, dry and heavy, with no glossy slime. 2001 Chinese coastal town realism, practical photography / game texture reference, restrained contrast, no labels, no text, no generic ocean imagery, no blue underwater look.
