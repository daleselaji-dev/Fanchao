# W01 Return Wall Hero Gate

Question: can the return-tide wall read as a real 2002 hotel wall undergoing one continuous material and geometric failure, without separate puddle/blob/plate primitives?

Status: v03 is the retained direction, not a finished asset. It replaces separate puddle/blob/plate primitives with one scanned, continuously deformed wall surface. v04's countable subsurface ribs were rejected because they read as fingers or a radiator.

v05 increased hydrostatic depth and added two converging structural folds. It was rejected before engine integration: the final silhouette still read immediately as giant fingers/ribs, so greater amplitude only amplified the wrong idea.

Next gate: v06 tests a different material event. A large, irregular paint-and-plaster skin separates along a torn line, exposes a black wet substrate and sags under retained water. The sheet must have a readable thin edge and back face; it must not read as an oval blister, relief sculpture or attached sea decoration.

v06 proved the thin edge and exposed backing can read, but its full-width smooth peel looked like a decorative wallpaper ribbon. v07 keeps the material construction and localises the failure: attached perimeter, one heavy retained-water belly, and only several jagged sections of the tear lifting away from dense mineral-and-mould substrate.
